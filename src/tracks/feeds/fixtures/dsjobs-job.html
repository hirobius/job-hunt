This role sits within Pennylane's design team, growing and maintaining Purse, its design system. You'll own component-level craft: building tokens, components, and patterns used by 40+ designers and engineers across web, mobile, and tablet. You'll partner with engineering, product designers, and the Design System Manager on long-term direction.

What you'll do

  • Build and maintain components, patterns, and tokens in Purse across web, mobile, and tablet
  • Write and maintain component documentation for designers, engineers, and AI tools
  • Run audits, support product designers, and turn recurring needs into systemic improvements

What we're looking for

  • Hands-on experience contributing to a design system at scale: components, variants, APIs, tokens, Figma libraries
  • Strong understanding of design token systems, semantic naming, theming / multi-brand patterns
  • Skilled at running design system audits and spotting inconsistencies in product surfaces
  • Experience writing component documentation clear for designers, engineers, and product managers
  • Expertise in making design systems interpretable by AI: structured content, machine-readable patterns, AI-ready docs
  • Solid grounding in accessibility (WCAG) and inclusive design practices
  • Able to slice an ambitious vision into small intermediate milestones
  • Pragmatic, knows when a good enough solution beats a perfect one
  • Curious about how design systems are evolving: AI, codegen, token automation, MCP
  • Enjoys switching between deep craft work and system-level decisions
  • Strong technical acumen in how design systems are implemented in code
  • Comfortable in a production-first environment where code is the source of truth
  • Experience partnering with engineering for design–code parity and smooth implementation
  • Experience supporting product designers via office hours, support model, or peer review
  • Ability to evangelize a design system and present rationale to stakeholders
  • Ability to handle complex support requests and turn them into permanent improvements
  • Energized by an ever-shifting work environment
  • Highly collaborative within team and with other stakeholders
  • Sufficiently experienced to prioritize business-led actions day to day

Nice to have

  • Experience with B2B SaaS products or fintech environments
  • Familiarity with multi-language / internationalization design challenges
  • Experience contributing to or maintaining a tokens pipeline (Style Dictionary, Tokens Studio, MCP-based pipelines)
  • Background in accessibility standards and inclusive design practices
  • Experience working in fast-growing scale-ups

Good to know

  • Must communicate well in both English and French
