1. The problem in numbers

  • 11 ways to find a job and 8 ways to find or keep a company, spread over two tabs, two secondary views, a Refine disclosure, a Calibrate disclosure and Manage track.
  • 13 synced fields in the blob (v11), plus 3 curation tables, plus 4 server tables that were never deployed.
  • Companies is three different things: watched board tiles, outreach targets and dream companies, each with its own store, plus a fourth derived "Company page".
  • Filtering is four overlapping layers: title filter, must include (with related titles), Calibrate (leave-out, level, pay) and the track scorer, which replaces some of the others on one track only.
  • Discovery code is about 16k of the app's 35.6k lines (non-test).

3. Design A: Server inbox

backend-deepsigned-in only

The server does the finding, ranking and remembering. One sources table (boards, companies, feeds, searches) feeds one ranked Inbox (the postings table), scored by sync-postings twice a day. One profile row replaces tracks. The client is thin and learns three calls:

useInbox()           → queue, review, sources
find(input)          → board | company | search | job
triage(id, save|skip|undo)

Deletes: all client discovery (Discover, Companies, Live postings, src/feed/ fetching, HN/Remotive/SAM sources, curation, tracks). CSP shrinks to Supabase only. About −15k source lines.

Migration: watched tiles, dreams, targets and saved searches move to sources; the active track's titles, must include, location and mode become profile. Undeployed migrations are rewritten in place. Blob v12 keeps jobs, documents, kit, trash.

Risks: (1) no discovery at all when signed out; (2) everything rests on an edge function that has never run (bundling, Ashby time limits, boards blocking server IPs); (3) generic ranking for non-design profiles; (4) abuse and cost of user-entered server fetches; legal.ts needs re-approval.

4. Design B: One list, client-first

flexibleworks signed out

The Pipeline is home. A new Status, New, sits before Saved: Find writes results into the same Job list. Every feeder (boards, dreams, targets, tiles, HN, SAM) becomes one Source in one synced store; one app-wide Filter replaces titles, sweep, must include, Calibrate. A PostingReader seam has two adapters: the browser (today's fetchFeed) and the server (postings rows).

Deletes: Discover, Companies, Live postings UI, tiles and curation, tracks, sweep, saved searches. Keeps every source and probe behind the seam. About −10k lines, +2.2k new.

Migration: tiles, dreams, targets become Sources (tag = old group); all tracks' titles merge into one Filter; set-aside tiles become dismissed fingerprints. New jobs ride in a separate found field so old clients never turn them into Saved.

Risks: (1) New jobs bury the real pipeline (needs caps and auto-expiry); (2) blob size; (3) no forward guard today: an older tab rewrites the blob at v11 and drops new fields; (4) one Filter for all hunts.

5. Design C: Today deck

common case first10-minute day

Home is Today: three counts in a fixed order, each opening a one-card-at-a-time walk. One Search profile per account (titles, must include, leave out, location, mode). Companies are followed automatically when you save one of their jobs. One Feed module with two adapters (server signed in, browser signed out). Everything else sits under More.

useDeck()  → card, left, save(), skip(), notThisCompany(), undo()
compileProfile(profile) → scorer config   // shared with the edge function
today(jobs, deckLeft, now) → review, apply, followUp

Deletes: Discover, Companies, Live postings, Find boards, GitHub, YC, Adele, ATS and big-board links, sweep, Calibrate, tracks, curation, Contracts. About −14k source lines, −11k test lines, +1.5k new.

Migration: blob v12 adds profile, companies, decided. Profile comes from the scored track (else the busiest). Companies = union of watched boards, dreams, targets with a board, and boards of saved jobs. Evidence becomes a Note document. "Download old setup" offered once.

Risks: (1) several tracks flatten into one; (2) empty deck on day one; (3) the edge function reads the blob's shape; (4) profile quality outside design systems.

7. Recommendation, as amended: C with no loss of breadth

Adrian picked C on 2026-10-28 on one hard condition: no loss of breadth. He wants as many distinct design-system openings as possible, in a list he can click through and apply from, not the same four jobs every day. This section is the amended design.

  1. Home = Today: Review new, Apply, Follow up, under the line "N new today from M sources", then New companies with Follow / Ignore (Q6). Jobs (the Pipeline) stays a top tab beside Today (amendment 2).
  2. Review new has two views of the same Feed. Browse (default) is a list: sort by newest, company or score; filter by text, source and work mode; open any posting; Save or Skip inline. Deck is the one-card option for a quick pass. The two views share one list and one set of verdicts.
  3. One Find box at the top of Today. Paste anything: a job link saves a Job (works signed out); a board or company link or name becomes a Source; words add titles to the Search profile.
  4. One Sources list in the synced blob: boards of every kind, followed companies (with or without a board) and feeds. Saving a job follows its company. Dreams become a star on a Source. Add sources helpers fill it: Check a list (paste or YC batch), YC suggestions, GitHub design-system orgs and the Adele seed. They are the same code as today in a smaller sheet.
  5. One Search profile: titles (the wide sweep runs over all of them), related titles, must include, leave out, location, mode, pay floor and level. The design-systems scorer stays as it is (it may sort, never hide; amendment 3). Stored as profiles[] with one entry, so a second hunt can come back later without a migration.
  6. One Feed reading every source there is today. It reads HN Who is hiring and Freelancer, Remotive, Jobicy and the designsystems.jobs RSS. It reads every watched board kind: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor and Personio. It has two adapters: the browser (today's fetchFeed, signed out) and the server (postings, signed in). The designsystems.jobs RSS is read on the server, as today; reading it in the browser too would need a CSP and legal.ts entry, so that waits for a separate decision. sync-postings reads Sources and the profile from the blob, so there is one source of truth and watched_companies / skips are never deployed.
  7. Search elsewhere: Big job boards (Remote Rocketship included) and the ATS Google search, as links built from the profile's titles and location, under Review.
  8. Skip hides that posting only. It is stored by posting fingerprint in decided and never touches the company. Not this company is its own action, in the posting's menu: it mutes the Source, and Undo or the Sources list unmutes it.

Breadth guard

Breadth is a test, not a promise. A recorded fixture holds a fixed set of inputs: the sources' answers (HN, Remotive, Jobicy, RSS and one board of each kind) and a profile with several titles and related titles on. Two counts run over it:

  • Before: today's pipeline (fetchFeed, wide sweep over every title, related titles, the scorer's Queue and Review). It counts distinct postings by fingerprint.
  • After: the new Feed with the same inputs.

The test fails if After < Before. The baseline number is pinned in the fixture, so a later change that drops a source or tightens a filter fails too until someone updates it on purpose. Today's "N new today from M sources" line counts from the same Feed, so a quiet day shows up as a number, not a feeling.

Why this mix. C's home is Adrian's three controls. The Browse list keeps the breadth and click-through he relies on, and the deck stays as a quick-pass option. One Sources list makes "companies are a property of jobs" true. The paste-anything Find box replaces four entry points (Add a company, Check a list, Add job, Watch a company) with one, and the helpers still feed it. What goes is the duplication: 19 entry points across two tabs, two secondary views, Refine, Calibrate and Manage track become 3 buttons, one paste box, one Sources list and one profile. The sources themselves all stay.

The data shape after:

blob v12
  jobs, documents, kit, trash, copydeck (legacy)   kept
  profiles[1]   titles, related, mustInclude, leaveOut,
                payFloor, level, location, mode
  sources[]     { kind: board|company|feed, ref, label,
                  star?, muted? }
  decided[]     { fingerprint, verdict: saved|skipped, at }
                newest 5,000 (never a company)
server
  tracker (blob)   postings   sync_log      // nothing else

Migration from v11 (one-time, after sync settles, like SnippetMigration):

  • The profile comes from the scored track, else the track with the most saved jobs. It takes its sweep titles, related titles, must include, and the newest saved search's leave out, pay and level. The notice lists the other tracks' titles; they are not merged.
  • Sources are the union across all tracks of: watched-board tiles, dreams (starred), targets, found GitHub teams the user saved, and the boards of saved jobs. They are deduped by company key. Turned-on feeds stay on.
  • Set-aside tiles and saved jobs seed decided. Evidence text becomes a Note document.
  • Non-board tiles (Apply now sections, curated search links) are offered once as a "Download your links (.txt)" file, then dropped.
  • Dropped after conversion: filters, sweeps, modes, targets, dreams, mustInclude (NAICS goes with SAM.gov), triage and evidence. The curation tables are frozen and dropped 30 days later.
  • Before any of this, a forward guard: today an older tab can rewrite the blob at v11 and silently drop newer fields.

Size, re-estimated: the old screens go (DiscoverView, DiscoverHeader, CompaniesPanel, LiveFeedPanel, TileGrid, ManageTrackDialog, SweepTitles, DreamCompanies, ScoredQueue, FindBoardsPanel and AdeleSeed shells), along with curation, tracks and Contracts. That is roughly −7.5k lines. Browse, the deck, the Find box, the Add sources sheet and the profile form add roughly +2.5k. The net is about −5k. Everything in src/feed/ and src/tracks/ stays behind the Feed seam.

7b. Amendments 2 and 3, Q5 and Q6: auto-scan, breadth first, preview, Jobs, no Companies tab

Adrian's decisions relayed on 2026-10-28. Where they conflict with section 7, this section wins.

Auto-scan replaces every manual discovery control

One Scan does all discovery. It runs twice a day on the server cron and once on the client when the app opens (when the last scan is older than an hour). Each run does four things:

  • Finds companies from every helper: YC hiring, GitHub design-system orgs and the Adele list. It also re-checks the names from any earlier pasted list.
  • Probes for boards for every company without one, across all eight board kinds, with the identity check below.
  • Reads every followed company's board and the feeds (HN, Remotive, Jobicy, the designsystems.jobs RSS), over every profile title (the old sweep).
  • Scores every posting and re-checks saved jobs' boards (the old liveness check).

The only UI left is Scan now and "Last scanned 6:23". These all go: YC batch, Check a list, Find teams, Suggest companies, the Adele button, "Check open roles at N companies", "Check dream companies", Live postings' fetch, and the sweep chips. Signed out, the client scan does the same work in the browser, within the existing rate budgets (GitHub, Remotive, probe).

Companies: no tab (Adrian's Q6)

The Companies top tab goes, so the final tabs are Today and Jobs. A company the scan finds shows up in New companies on Today, with Follow or Ignore, and its matching jobs are already in Review new. Ignore mutes it so the scan never brings it back. Leaving it alone keeps its jobs visible until the user decides. Followed and Ignored companies live in a small Companies list under the Account menu, with Unfollow and Un-ignore. That list is the Sources list; there is no other company screen. The derived Company page, reached from a job, stays.

One filter: is it a design-system role? (amendment 3)

Breadth first. The app's one job is to collect every design-system opening posted anywhere. The fit-score gates proposed earlier (Match / Maybe / No match) are dropped. A posting is listed when it passes two checks:

  • The inclusion check: its title or description names a design-system role ("design system(s)", "design technologist", "design system engineer" and so on). It uses the existing phrase rules in feed/mustInclude.ts (termRegex: whole words, "systems" matches "design system", "ecosystem" never matches, and phrases break at separators).
  • Adrian's own profile filters: leave out, location, mode, pay floor and level.

Anything that fails is counted as "N not design-system roles" (a link opens them once), so nothing disappears silently. The scorer (src/tracks/score.ts), Fit and screening stay exactly as they are. They may sort or badge a row, but they never hide one, and they get no new work in this plan. (Q5 later takes the Screening tab off the job page; its data is kept.)

On the server, the scan stores each posting's title and description in a Postgres tsvector column with a GIN index. The inclusion terms and leave-out terms become a websearch_to_tsquery pre-filter, and the same phrase rules then confirm each row. No AI, no API key.

Breadth guard, restated: it counts distinct postings that pass the inclusion check. If the new Feed and Scan ever list fewer than today's pipeline does from the same recorded inputs, the test fails.

Tap a job: an in-app preview

Tapping a row in Browse opens a short preview sheet: the title, company, location and pay, the source, the first lines of the description, and why it is listed (the phrase that matched). It has Save, Skip and Not this company. Open posting ↗ is one tap away. Back closes the sheet and returns to the same row.

Jobs stays a top tab, simplified hard

The tabs are Today · Jobs, and Jobs (the Pipeline) is no longer under More. Adrian's keep list (relayed 2026-10-28) settles the Jobs list: keep To apply with Start applying, Follow up, Next 7 days and the full job list (search, status, sort). Cut Getting started, and move Recently removed and Export links off the page. For the job page (Adrian's Q5): keep Details and Notes, turn Documents and Decks into plain lists of external links for now, and cut To-dos, Screening and the Interviews log from the page. Their data stays in storage and backups and is never deleted.

Bug: the board probe trusts a slug

The probe matched "Forward" (a YC company) to three different boards: Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters and Personio. probeCompany accepts every live board whose slug matches a candidate, so a common word maps to whichever company holds that slug on each ATS. The fix is an identity check. A hit counts only when the board's company website or domain matches the company's known website, or when a name match is confirmed by a domain. More than one ATS answering for a common-word slug makes the result unconfirmed, not found. Filed as its own bug; the auto-scan depends on it.

  2. Today home (#383). Three buttons and "N new today from M sources". The tabs become Today · Jobs.

  9. Simplify Jobs / Pipeline (#416). The proposal in 7b goes to Adrian first; only what he approves is built.
  10. Remove the old screens and manual controls (#389).
  11. Cleanup (#390).

Bug, separate from the slices: board probe identity check (#417).

9. What Adrian loses, and why it's worth it

  • More than one track. One Search profile at a time; profiles[] leaves room to switch later.
  • Curated tile boards (Apply now sections, Manage track). They are offered once as a links file.
  • SAM.gov contracts.
  • Calibrate as its own panel. Its knobs fold into the profile.
  • Manual discovery buttons. The scan does their work on a schedule; Scan now covers "I want it now".

Not lost:

  • every job source and board kind, plus the wide sweep and related titles
  • the scorer, Fit and screening, unchanged
  • a click-through list of every design-system posting found, with a preview
  • every company-finding helper (now automatic)
  • Search elsewhere
  • the liveness check

The breadth guard holds this.

Why it's worth it. The first question stops being "which of 19 tools do I use?" and becomes "what's new, what do I apply to, who do I follow up with?". Discovery runs by itself, the list arrives already scored, and breadth stays under test.
