Design-system job sources

Researched 2026-10-28. Full detail: docs/research/ds-job-sources.md. “Live” = checked from the build container that day.

The short version. Design-system jobs sit on company job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday) and on two niche boards that read those. General remote and design boards carry almost none: Remotive gave 0 of 10 for “design system” (live).

So getting every job means covering more companies, not adding more job boards.

Every source checked

Niche design-system boards
  • designsystems.jobs: 112 live, no terms page, robots allow all, server only. Add (#1)
  • Into Design Systems: 225–259, keyless agent connector. Ask first (#3)
  • designbetter.careers: a few dozen, web pages only. Link.
  • weloveproduct, @DSJobBoard, DS House, zeroheight/Supernova: none or dormant. Skip.
Design boards
  • Dribbble, Behance, Working Not Working, UXJobsBoard, IxDA, Smashing, Uxcel: under 10 each, no feed. Links only
  • Coroflot, Authentic Jobs: RSS, 0–3 each. Maybe later.
  • Krop: closed in 2024.
Remote boards
  • Remotive: 0 found (live). In app.
  • Jobicy, Remote OK: 0–3. In app.
  • Himalayas: 2–8, keyless, read terms first. #8.
  • We Work Remotely, 4dayweek, Working Nomads, The Muse: 0–3 each. Low value.
  • Arc, Remote.co, JustRemote, NoDesk, Arbeitnow: none. Skip.
Startup boards
  • Wellfound, Otta/Welcome to the Jungle, Built In, Levels.fyi: 3–15 each, no API, Wellfound bans bots. Links only
  • YC Work at a Startup: use the community company list (in app).
  • HN Jobs: 0 ever. Skip.
Aggregators
  • TheirStack: best title search, free 200/month. Add (#4)
  • Adzuna: free key, 2,500 calls a month, must show “Jobs by Adzuna”, commercial use needs a licence. Maybe.
  • Jooble, Careerjet: free keys, unclear terms. Maybe.
  • Fantastic.jobs: about $1 per 1,000 jobs. Cheapest paid.
  • Google Jobs resellers (SerpApi etc.): Google is suing SerpApi. Risky.
  • Not allowed: LinkedIn (incl. its guest search), Indeed, Glassdoor, scraping Google, JobSpy-style scrapers.
Company job-board systems (ATS)
  • Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby: keyless, browser-safe (live: Figma 142, Spotify 89, Ramp 172). In app and server.
  • Workable, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters: keyless. Browser only today.
  • Teamtailor: the app's path isn't documented anywhere; check it. Personio: add the documented /xml feed as a fallback.
  • Gem: public job board API, startup-heavy. #7.
  • BambooHR, Breezy, Pinpoint: keyless, small companies. Low.
  • Rippling, JazzHR, Dover, Polymer: no confirmed feed. Skip.
  • Workday: undocumented and bot-protected. Links only. iCIMS, Jobvite, Taleo, SuccessFactors: customer keys. Links only.
  • Finding companies: Common Crawl index (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Gem) and certificate logs (Recruitee, Teamtailor, BambooHR, Breezy, Personio), run offline, then confirm with the app's probe.
Community
  • HN “Who is hiring?”: 1–2 a month (live). In app. Freelancer thread: broken (#5).
  • Reddit: needs sign-in for the API; commercial use needs enterprise access. Under 5 a month. Skip.
  • Slack and Discord job channels: not allowed without the community's own bot.
  • Newsletters: no job listings found.
  • Bluesky search: keyless, low volume. Maybe as an experiment.
  • LinkedIn posts, X: not allowed.
  • GitHub design-system orgs: in app (company finder). awesome-design-systems: add as a seed (#2).
